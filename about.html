Publication

Pier Counsel Contributes the India Chapter to The Venture Capital Law Review

Pier Counsel authored the India chapter for The Venture Capital Law Review, Third Edition, published by Law Business Research. The chapter covers the legal and regulatory framework governing venture capital in India, including fund formation, fund management, investment structures, capital raising by startups, and exit mechanisms. It reflects the firm’s depth of expertise in the Indian startup and investment ecosystem and its recognition among leading legal practitioners globally.

Our Mission

To provide clear, structured, and commercially sound legal advisory that enables businesses to operate with confidence, manage risk effectively, and make informed decisions in complex regulatory environments.

Our Vision

To be a trusted legal partner for businesses across jurisdictions, known for integrity, precision, and a practical approach that aligns legal strategy with long-term business growth.
